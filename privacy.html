My videos RCIM

Privacy Policy

Effective from October 17, 2026

My videos RCIM is a private desktop application installed on a single computer and operated only by its developer. It turns long videos into short clips and publishes videos on YouTube channels owned by the developer, and on the channels of content creators who have authorized him to publish their own videos. This policy explains what data the application accesses and what it does with it.

Use of YouTube API Services

My videos RCIM uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the application to publish on your channel, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Who is responsible

The application is developed and operated by an independent developer based in France. For any question or request: alias.jack.id@gmail.com.

Google data accessed

When a channel owner signs in with Google, the application asks for three permissions, and only these:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: upload videos (with a scheduled publication time) and set their thumbnails on the authorized channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: read the basic information of the authorized channel (name and channel ID) and the list of its playlists, to confirm where each upload goes and find the right playlist.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube: required by YouTube to manage playlists. It is used only to create a playlist on the authorized channel when it does not exist yet, and to add the videos uploaded by the application to it. It is never used to edit or delete existing videos, playlists, comments or any other content.

No other data in the Google account is accessed: no email, contacts, files or history. The application does not read any channel other than the authorized one, does not collect any data about viewers, and does not change or delete videos already on the channel.

How the data is used

These permissions are used only to publish, on the authorized channel, the videos that the channel owner provided or approved for publication, and to add them to a playlist of that channel when requested. They are not used for anything else.

Storage

The authorization token issued by Google is stored in a local file on the developer’s computer, so that the channel owner does not need to sign in again for each upload. Videos, transcriptions and titles are also stored on that computer.

There is no server belonging to the developer: the application talks directly to Google’s interfaces. No data is collected, centralized or hosted anywhere other than on the developer’s computer and at Google.

Optional help from AI tools

Some titles and descriptions may be drafted with the help of third-party AI tools, then reviewed before publishing. Only the text needed for this purpose (such as the transcript or a summary of the video) is sent to these tools. No data obtained through Google or YouTube API Services is ever sent to them. A creator can ask that no AI tool be used for their videos.

Sharing with third parties

No data obtained through Google or YouTube API Services is transferred, sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training models.

My videos RCIM’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Withdrawing the authorization

Any channel owner can revoke access at any time, without contacting the developer, from the Google security settings page. Revocation takes effect immediately, and the application can no longer publish on that channel.

Data deletion

A channel owner can also ask for the deletion of their data by writing to alias.jack.id@gmail.com. The stored token, the channel information and any of their videos kept on the developer’s computer are then deleted within 7 days. The same deletion is made when a creator stops using the publishing service.

Security

Tokens are stored on a personal computer protected by a locked user session, which is not shared with anyone. The application folder is not copied to any medium accessible to third parties.

Children

The service is not intended for people under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the effective date at the top is changed.
