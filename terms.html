About the application

My videos RCIM is a private desktop application for Windows, developed and operated by FIDELE NTSO BASSIGOUAGA, an independent developer based in France. It is used only by its developer, on his own computer, to upload and schedule original videos to YouTube channels that he owns. The application is not sold, distributed or offered to any third party.

Use of YouTube API Services

My videos RCIM uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set thumbnails and identify the connected YouTube channel. By using My videos RCIM, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Data accessed through YouTube API Services is also handled in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and our own Privacy Policy.

Google account authorization

Each YouTube channel is connected to the application through Google OAuth 2.0. Authorization tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer only and are never shared with anyone.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Once access is revoked, the application can no longer upload or manage videos on that channel.

Acceptable use

  • The application is only used to upload content that the channel owner has the right to publish.
  • All uploaded content must comply with the YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube Community Guidelines.
  • The application does not collect, analyze, sell or share data about YouTube users or viewers, and it is not used for advertising.

Data deletion

Locally stored authorization tokens and settings can be deleted at any time by removing the channel from the application or by revoking access as described above. Deleting stored data stops all further access to the corresponding YouTube channel.
