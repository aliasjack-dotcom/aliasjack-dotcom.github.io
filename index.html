My videos RCIM

An hour of video rarely holds an hour of highlights.

My videos RCIM transcribes a long video, finds the passages that stand on their own, turns them into subtitled vertical clips, and publishes them on YouTube.

Source videoKept passages

What the application does

My videos RCIM is a desktop application for Windows. It is installed on a single computer and works on the video files stored there.

  • The soundtrack is transcribed, with a timestamp for each word.
  • The strongest passages are found and cut on complete sentences.
  • Each clip is reframed to vertical format and subtitled. Titles and descriptions can be drafted with the help of AI tools, then reviewed before publishing.
  • Clips and ready-made videos are uploaded to the right YouTube channel at scheduled times.

Who uses it

The application is privately owned and operated by its developer only. It is not sold, distributed or made available to anyone else.

It publishes videos on YouTube channels owned by the developer, and on the channels of content creators who entrust him with their own videos and authorize him to publish them. Each creator keeps full ownership of their channel and of their videos, and can withdraw this authorization at any time. This service is currently free of charge.

Why it needs access to YouTube

My videos RCIM uses YouTube API Services. Only the publishing step needs an authorization, given by the owner of each channel through Google sign-in. The application asks for two permissions: uploading a video to the authorized channel, and reading that channel’s basic information to confirm where the upload went.

It does not access any other data in the Google account, does not read any other channel, and does not change or delete existing videos.

Nothing is sent to a server of ours: the application talks directly to Google from the developer’s computer.
